The ticket says: *Carla can't borrow anything. The website says she has reached her limit of
three books. She has none.* The developer tries it: a new member, a loan, it works. Three
loans, a fourth refused, correct. Every test is green. *Cannot reproduce*, and the ticket
waits for more information that never comes.

Bugs like this depend on history, a sequence of things that happened to one record over
weeks. Nobody can guess the sequence, and a database row doesn't keep it. Every snippet
here runs in
[`examples/blog/wont-reproduce.test.ts`](https://github.com/davidnussio/effect-cqrs/blob/main/examples/blog/wont-reproduce.test.ts).

<Aside type="caution">
A stream can hold personal data: names, addresses, notes. Leave it out or replace it before it
leaves production. With [personal data fields](../../guides/personal-data/), `history`
returns decrypted values, so the export script is the place to redact them.
</Aside>

## Carla's account fixes itself

There is no data to repair. Carla's stream says what happened, and it was right all along:
she asked, the books were out, the requests were cancelled. The member's state, the one the
limit check reads, is folded from those events every time, so the moment the fix is
deployed, her state counts zero open loans and her next request goes through. The same goes
for every other member who hit it, whether or not they filed a ticket.

With an `open_loans` counter in a table, the fix would also need a migration to recompute the
counter for everyone, from data that may not say what it should be.

## Rules of thumb

- **Ask for the aggregate id, not for steps to reproduce.** The stream *is* the steps.
- **Keep a support script that exports a stream as JSON**, encoded with the events' own
  schema, and redacting personal data.
- **Turn every such bug into a fixture with the real history.** It's the shortest
  reproduction there is, and it keeps the bug from coming back.
- **When a change adds a state, test the commands that read it.** Here a new status,
  `cancelled`, changed what the limit check counted, and no test asked for a loan after a
  cancellation.
